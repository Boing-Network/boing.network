# Boing brand foundation

This file is the working reference for how Boing is named, described and shown across **boing.network**, **boing.express**, **boing.observer** and **boing.finance**. Keep copy in all four repositories consistent with it. If a product needs an exception, change this file first.

> **Status rule.** Boing is on a **public testnet. Mainnet is not live.** Independent audits are **not** complete. Never write copy that says or implies otherwise. Treat testnet status as a strength: explain it, do not hide it.

## 1. Positioning

- **Name:** Boing (kept).
- **Core line:** Boing is an independent Layer 1 where quality is enforced by the protocol.
- **Supporting line:** A decentralized, high-performance blockchain built from first principles, with transparent, protocol-level quality assurance.
- **Tagline:** Boing Network — Quality, enforced by the protocol.
- **Direction:** from "a playful blockchain with many features" to "verifiable infrastructure with a distinctive personality".

## 2. Brand promise, personality and principles

**Promise.** Boing makes on-chain participation more verifiable by enforcing quality checks at the protocol layer.

**Personality.** Rigorous, not cold. Open, not naive. Bold, not exaggerated. Playful, not unserious. Experimental, not careless.

**Principles.**

1. Explain before promoting.
2. Evidence before claims.
3. Treat testnet status as a strength.
4. Make rules understandable to non-engineers.
5. Never confuse decentralization with the absence of standards.

## 3. Naming system

Use these names consistently. Do not switch between "Boing", "Boing Network" and "Boing blockchain" in one document.

| Product | Role | Descriptor | One-line statement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boing Network** | Core protocol and ecosystem | The quality-assured Layer 1 | Boing Network is an independent Layer 1 where quality is enforced by the protocol. |
| **Boing Express** | Wallet and account access | Your gateway to Boing | A non-custodial wallet designed for the Boing ecosystem. |
| **Boing Observer** | Explorer and transparency layer | See what happens on-chain | An explorer with integrated QA transparency. |
| **Boing Finance** | DeFi and liquidity app | Move liquidity across chains | A cross-chain liquidity interface spanning Boing and external networks. |

- Write product names in title case in prose ("Boing Finance"). Domain names (`boing.finance`) appear only as URLs or in technical contexts.
- "Boing" alone is fine for the chain in running prose after "Boing Network" has been introduced ("Build on Boing").
- Do not define a product by what it is not (no "not MetaMask", no "not an Etherscan clone").

## 4. Boing Quality Gate

**Name:** Boing Quality Gate.

**Approved definition.** The Boing Quality Gate screens deployments against transparent rules before they reach the ledger. Automated checks handle clear cases; a decentralized community pool reviews edge cases.

**Approved framing.** Designed to reduce malicious, deceptive and low-quality deployments through transparent, protocol-level checks.

**Approved comparison.** Boing introduces protocol-level deployment checks designed to identify and restrict deployments that fail transparent quality criteria before they are accepted on-chain.

**Never say** "no scams", "scam-proof", "scam-free", or anything that implies all malicious activity is prevented. Never say "No other major chain..." or "the first L1 to...".

## 5. Message hierarchy

**Primary:** Quality assurance, enforced by the protocol.

**Secondary**

1. **Secure by design:** Rust, HotStuff proof of stake, Ed25519, BLAKE3.
2. **Open participation:** permissionless for users, developers and validators.
3. **Transparent by default:** observable rules, deployments and outcomes.
4. **Built for real applications:** native accounts, adaptive gas, cross-chain liquidity.

**Proof points:** purpose-built VM rather than EVM compatibility; ~2-second target blocks; public testnet and faucet; open validator and node participation; automated QA screening with community review for unclear cases; explorer-level transparency around QA decisions.

## 6. Audience messages

| Audience | Message | Supporting points |
|---|---|---|
| **Users** | A safer way to explore on-chain applications. | Create a wallet, get testnet BOING, send and receive, explore accounts, blocks and transactions, connect to apps. |
| **Developers** | Build on an independent chain with native account and application primitives. | JSON-RPC, Boing SDK, purpose-built VM, native account abstraction, adaptive gas, session keys, social recovery, deployment QA requirements, clear testnet docs. |
| **Validators and node operators** | Help operate an open network with transparent participation rules. | Rust, HotStuff, public testnet, node setup, validator registration, slashing and appeals, launch-partner pathway, mainnet readiness milestones. |
| **Institutions** (later, low emphasis) | Transparent, rule-based infrastructure for applications that require verifiable deployment standards. | Designed for verifiability and institutional review. |

Do **not** write "regulators can trust us" or imply institutional trust is established. Audits, genesis, incident response and mainnet readiness remain open.

## 7. Tone

Engineering-first, direct, confident but evidence-led, open about testnet status, occasionally playful, never hype-dependent.

- Playful "bounce" lines ("A better bounce for blockchain", "Built to bounce forward", "bounces back") belong in campaigns, onboarding and community content only, never as the central message.
- Prefer verbs and facts ("checks", "screens", "publishes") over superlatives ("revolutionary", "unstoppable", "best").
- Never promise returns, yields or guaranteed safety.

## 9. Visual direction

- **Foundation:** dark, deep navy. One energetic accent. Rounded geometric forms inspired by motion. Strong grid, diagrams and technical illustration.
- **Mascot:** onboarding, empty states, status messages and community material. Keep docs, validator tools, explorer data views and institutional pages restrained.
- **Logo ("Arc Slab" family):** a rounded navy slab with a blue gradient outline, the Boing recovery arc and a glowing orb. It is a simple, scalable symbol that works as a favicon and wallet icon, and it references motion and validation. Avoid the generic chain link, coin, shield and rocket. All four products share the slab, palette and orb, and each has its own variant so the family is recognisable but the products are distinguishable at a glance.

  | Product | Mark variant | Wordmark |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Boing Express | Full Arc Slab with two speed streaks, strong arc and glow (the reference mark). | "Boing Express", Comfortaa Bold, all blue. |
  | Boing Network | Arc Slab without the speed streaks: clean slab, strong arc, glowing orb. | "Boing Network", Comfortaa Bold, all blue. |
  | Boing Observer | Observer variant: flatter scan arc ending in an orb with a dark pupil, inside a segmented aperture ring, so it reads as "observing". No streaks. | "Boing Observer", Comfortaa Bold, all blue. |
  | Boing Finance | Slab with a fainter arc and an orb ring (no streaks, no glow). Kept as shipped. | Lowercase "boing.finance", light-to-blue gradient. |

- **Variant rule:** keep the 64-unit slab (`rect x6 y6 52 x 52, rx 14`), the navy gradient, the blue frame gradient and the orb gradient identical. Vary only the accent elements (streaks, arc curvature, orb ring or aperture). New products get their own variant; do not reuse another product's mark. A change to a shared element (slab, palette) must be made in all four repositories together.
- **Wordmark rule:** the product name in Comfortaa Bold, title case, set all in Boing blue (`#3B8CFF`) for Express, Network and Observer. On hover the text lightens to `#8DB8FF`. Do not set these names in white. Boing Finance keeps its lowercase domain-style wordmark.
- **Source files (this repo):** `website/public/logo.svg` (full Network mark), `website/public/favicon.svg` (simplified 32-unit mark for 16 and 32 px), `website/public/assets/boing-network-wordmark.svg` (mark plus outlined wordmark, used for the OG card), `desktop-hub/public/favicon.svg` (Desktop Hub uses the Network mark, and `src-tauri/icons` is generated from it with `npm run tauri icon public/favicon.svg`). Rasters (favicons, `favicon.ico`, Apple touch icon, PWA icons, `og.png`) are generated by `npm run generate:brand-raster` in `website/` and committed. Express, Observer and Finance keep their own sources in their repositories.
